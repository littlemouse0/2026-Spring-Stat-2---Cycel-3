Question 1: Gender and Current Cigarette Use
============================================================

Research question:
Is the proportion of current cigarette use different between male and female students?

Variables:
- Group variable: Gender, based on WhatIsYourSex
- Response variable: Current_Cigarette_Binary, based on CurrentCigaretteUse
- Current_Cigarette_Binary: 1 = current cigarette use, 0 = no current cigarette use

Hypotheses:
H0: The proportion of current cigarette use is the same for male and female students.
HA: The proportion of current cigarette use is different between male and female students.

Descriptive statistics:
        No_Current_Cigarette_Use  Current_Cigarette_Use  Total  Current_Cigarette_Proportion
Gender                                                                                      
Female                      5573                   1167   6740                      0.173145
Male                        5154                   1418   6572                      0.215764

Two-proportion z-test results:
Female proportion: 0.1731
Male proportion: 0.2158
Difference, Female - Male: -0.0426
Z statistic: -6.2148
P-value: 0.0000
95% CI for difference: (-0.0561, -0.0292)

Chi-square test results:
Chi-square statistic: 38.3521
Degrees of freedom: 1
P-value: 0.0000

Conclusion:
Reject the null hypothesis. There is statistically significant evidence that the proportion of current cigarette use differs between male and female students.
